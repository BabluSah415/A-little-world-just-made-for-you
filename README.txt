====================================================================
   BIRTHDAY SURPRISE WEBSITE FOR SUCHITA — SIMPLE USER GUIDE
====================================================================

Hello! You do NOT need to edit any HTML, CSS, or JavaScript code
to add your own photographs or birthday music. Just follow these
easy steps:

--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHERE TO PUT YOUR THREE PHOTOGRAPHS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Open the "assets" folder inside "Suchita-Birthday-Website".

--------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT FILENAMES TO USE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Your three photographs MUST be named:
  - photo1.jpg
  - photo2.jpg
  - photo3.jpg

(Tip: If your photos are .png, .jpeg, or .webp instead of .jpg,
that ALSO works automatically — just name them photo1.png,
photo2.png, photo3.png and delete the old .jpg files!)

--------------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHERE TO PUT THE BIRTHDAY MUSIC (MP3)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Copy your favourite MP3 song file.
2. Rename it to:
     birthday-music.mp3
3. Paste it inside the "assets" folder (replacing the existing
   birthday-music.mp3 file).

The music will automatically start playing as soon as Suchita
enters the secret password (0612) and unlocks the surprise!

--------------------------------------------------------------------
4. HOW TO REPLACE EXISTING PHOTOGRAPHS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Copy your 3 chosen photos of Suchita.
2. Rename them photo1.jpg, photo2.jpg, and photo3.jpg.
3. Paste them into the "assets" folder.
4. When your computer/phone asks "Replace existing files?", click YES.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
5. HOW TO TEST THE WEBSITE ON YOUR COMPUTER OR PHONE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- On a Computer: Double-click "index.html" to open it in Chrome,
  Edge, or Safari.
- Test the Secret Password: Type 0612 and tap "Unlock Your Surprise".
- Test the 3 Photos: Swipe or click the arrows on Page 8 to confirm
  all three photos appear clearly.
- Test the Music: Make sure your device volume is up and check the
  "Playing" / "Music" button at the top-right corner.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
6. HOW TO PUBLISH THE WEBSITE ONLINE FOR FREE (NO CODING NEEDED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Option A — Netlify Drop (Easiest, takes 30 seconds):
  1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop in your web browser.
  2. Drag and drop the entire "Suchita-Birthday-Website" folder
     onto the page.
  3. Netlify will give you a live website link immediately that you
     can share with Suchita on WhatsApp or Instagram!

Option B — GitHub Pages (Free forever):
  1. Create a free account on https://github.com and make a new
     public repository.
  2. Upload all files and the "assets" folder from
     "Suchita-Birthday-Website".
  3. Open Repository Settings -> Pages -> Source: "main" branch ->
     Save. Your free link will be ready in ~1 minute.
